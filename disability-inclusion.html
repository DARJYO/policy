Disability Inclusion

First created: May 27, 2019

Last updated: January 08, 2025

DARJYO structured policy outline

This announcement is intended to inform recipients of DARJYO (Pty) Ltd. issued documents about our verification process implemented to ensure document authenticity and security.

1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to promote an inclusive environment for individuals with disabilities, ensuring they have equal opportunities to participate in organizational activities without facing discrimination or barriers.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all programs, activities, and services offered by DARJYO, including but not limited to events, training sessions, and community engagement initiatives.

3. Definitions

  • Disability: A substantial restriction in the capacity of a person to carry out daily activities due to physical, sensory, mental health, or intellectual impairments.
  • Reasonable Accommodation: Adjustments made to enable individuals with disabilities to participate fully in organizational activities.

    • 4. Principles of Inclusion

  • Equal Access: Ensure that all facilities and services are accessible to individuals with disabilities.
  • Respect for Diversity: Acknowledge and respect the diverse needs and abilities of individuals with disabilities.
  • Participation: Actively encourage the participation of individuals with disabilities in all organizational activities.

    • 5. Implementation Strategies

  • Accessibility Audits: Regularly assess the accessibility of facilities and materials used in organizational activities.
  • Training and Awareness: Provide training for staff and volunteers on disability awareness and inclusion practices.
  • Communication: Ensure that all communication materials are available in accessible formats (e.g., Braille, large print, electronic formats).
  • Feedback Mechanism: Establish a process for individuals with disabilities to provide feedback on accessibility and inclusion efforts.

    • 6. Reasonable Accommodations

    *Provide necessary adjustments such as: **Accessible venues for meetings and events. **Assistive devices or technologies as needed. **Flexible scheduling or alternative formats for training materials.

    7. Partnerships

    Collaborate with organizations that specialize in disability advocacy to enhance inclusion efforts and share best practices.

    8. Monitoring and Evaluation

  • Regularly review the effectiveness of the policy through surveys and feedback from participants with disabilities.
  • Adjust the policy as necessary based on evaluation outcomes.

    • 9. Compliance

    This policy aligns with national legislation regarding disability rights, including the Employment Equity Act and the Code of Good Practice on Persons with Disabilities15. It also reflects international standards set by organizations such as the International Labour Organization (ILO) regarding disability inclusion3.

    10. Conclusion

    DARJYO is committed to fostering an inclusive environment where individuals with disabilities can thrive. By implementing this policy, we aim to break down barriers and promote equal opportunities for all participants. This framework serves as a foundation for promoting disability inclusion within DARJYO's organizational activities while ensuring compliance with relevant laws and best practices.
